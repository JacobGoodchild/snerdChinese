# Data licences

The files in this folder are generated by `scripts/build-data.mjs` from:

- **HSK word lists:** [drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary), MIT licence. Definitions come from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Character decompositions, etymologies and stroke graphics (`chars/`):** [skishore/makemeahanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi).
  `dictionary.txt` data is under the GNU LGPL. `graphics.txt` stroke data comes from Arphic PL KaitiM GB and UKai fonts under the
  [Arphic Public License](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/master/APL/LICENSE).
- **Example sentences (`ex/`):** [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR. Each sentence links back to its Tatoeba page.
  Sentences originally written in traditional characters were converted to simplified with OpenCC and are marked in the app.
  Pinyin for the sentences is generated with pinyin-pro.
